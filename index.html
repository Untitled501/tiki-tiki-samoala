The tragic tale

Chapter 1

Once upon a time

Tiki Tiki Samoala lived a perfect life. He had a beach, a flower behind his ear, a nice necklace, and 4,000 coconuts in savings. Life was tiki. Life was samoala.

Chapter 2

The investment

A seagull named Gerald told him about SkibidiCoin. "It can only go up," said Gerald. Tiki's eyes got very big. Well, they were already very big. They got bigger.

Chapter 3

It did not go up

SkibidiCoin dropped 100% in eleven minutes. Tiki's whole world turned upside down. Gerald flew away and has not been seen since.

Chapter 4

The loan

He borrowed 50 coconuts from Crabbo Gambino, the most dangerous crab on the island. The interest rate is "one pinch per day." The beach has not looked the same since.

Chapter 5

The termites

Then the termites showed up. They looked at the palm trees. Then they looked at Tiki. Tiki is made of wood. Tiki has not slept since Tuesday.

Chapter 6

The buffet ban

He was arrested at the all-you-can-eat pineapple buffet for "eating all of it." He says that was the whole point of the buffet. He smiled in his mugshot. He had no choice.

Chapter 7

And now, you

The beach is gray. The waves are sad. Tiki has one coconut and his dignity left, and he is willing to sell the dignity. Please help before Crabbo comes back.
